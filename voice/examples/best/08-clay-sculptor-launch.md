Clay just launched the feature that might get it from $3B -> $10B.

It solves the biggest problem that users have faced to date- actually learning the product.

Their new Sculptor feature lets you use natural language to build Clay tables. Think Lovable, but for Clay.

I personally think this is a huge step for the business and arguably their most important feature to date (maybe besides Claygent). It's going to be massive for adoption, and going to make the tool so much more friendly for beginners than before.

I've had a few people ask how I think this will affect the Clay Agency ecosystem- I actually think it could improve it. It cuts out the agencies that could just "use Clay" and bolsters the need for those who can thoughtfully implement the tool into a broader GTM system, particularly at larger companies.

Super interested to hear all of your thoughts on this- it's not 100% there yet imo, but quickly getting there. Very exciting stuff, and massive congrats to the team for accomplishing such an engineering feat!
